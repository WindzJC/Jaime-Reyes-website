THE PERSON BEHIND THE STORIES

2017
THE YEAR THE UNFINISHED STORY COULD NO LONGER WAIT

He had lived a remarkable life.
Then he nearly lost it.

Jaime Reyes was born in Puerto Rico, served in Vietnam, ran a business and spent 25 years with Philadelphia’s Sheriff’s Office. He had also carried a story about the origins of religious belief for years without finishing it.

In a published interview, Reyes described a near-death experience in May 2017 and a prolonged recovery. Returning home, he decided it was time to put that story on paper.

IN HIS OWN ACCOUNT

He completed the first draft of In the Beginning in three weeks, then spent months revising it.

02 / PERSONAL NONFICTIONA SECOND BODY OF WORK

THE LAZARUS SYNDROME / WHY CAN’T I DIE?

What happens after a life is declared over?

Accounts of spontaneous resuscitation, near-death experiences, and one man’s extraordinary personal history lie at the heart of Jaime Reyes’s second book.

In The Lazarus Syndrome, Reyes draws on reported revival cases and his own experiences. It is an inquiry shaped by personal testimony and medical questions—not a clinical reference or medical guidance.
