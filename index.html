THE PERSON BEHIND THE STORIES

2017

THE YEAR THE UNFINISHED MANUSCRIPT COULD NO LONGER WAIT

A story carried for years.
A reason to finish it.

Born in Arecibo, Puerto Rico, Jaime Reyes has lived through military service in Vietnam, business ownership and a 25-year career with Philadelphia’s Sheriff’s Office. He returned to college later in life, earning his degree in two years through examinations and recognition of prior learning, as he describes on his author website.

He had written articles and short stories long before publishing a novel. In a published interview, Reyes recalled a near-death experience in May 2017, followed by a prolonged recovery. It convinced him to finish the story that became In the Beginning.

IN HIS OWN ACCOUNT

He completed the first draft of In the Beginning in three weeks, then spent months revising it.

THE LAZARUS SYNDROME / WHY CAN’T I DIE?

What happens after a life is declared over?

Reported spontaneous resuscitations, near-death experiences and Jaime Reyes’s own accounts of survival form the center of his nonfiction work.

The Lazarus Syndrome also draws on episodes from his life, including memories of the Vietnam War. These personal accounts and interpretations invite discussion; they are not clinical evidence or medical advice.
