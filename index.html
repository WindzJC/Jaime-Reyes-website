THE BEGINNING OF BELIEF

The storm passed.
Authority remained.

A powerful storm. A frightened tribe. One man who understands that fear can become influence.

In In the Beginning: The Early Days of Religious Beliefs, Og, an older Neanderthal, uses his knowledge of storms to persuade Chief Olo and the tribe that he can appease the spirits. It is the first act in a family legacy of religious authority.

THE LAZARUS SYNDROME

When the heart stops,
what comes next?

Reported returns of circulation. Near-death experiences. A personal account of survival.

In The Lazarus Syndrome: Why Can’t I Die?, Reyes explores spontaneous resuscitation, reported near-death and out-of-body experiences, and episodes from his own life. The book also draws on his years in Vietnam and law enforcement.

03 / THE AUTHOR

Jaime Reyes.

Vietnam veteran. Public servant. Writer.

The story had waited for years. In 2017, Reyes decided it could wait no longer.

Born in Puerto Rico, Jaime Reyes moved to the mainland United States at eight. He went on to serve in Vietnam, run a business, and work for 25 years in the Philadelphia Sheriff’s Office. Along the way, he wrote articles and opinion pieces and earned a bachelor’s degree later in life.

After a serious medical crisis and recovery in 2017, he committed to completing the novel he had been developing for years: In the Beginning. His later nonfiction, The Lazarus Syndrome, examines reported revivals alongside experiences from his own life.
